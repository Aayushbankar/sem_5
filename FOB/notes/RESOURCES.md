## Official docs & specs

- Ethereum docs: https://ethereum.org/en/developers/docs/
- Solidity docs: https://docs.soliditylang.org
- Hyperledger Fabric docs: https://hyperledger-fabric.readthedocs.io
- EIP-20 (ERC-20): https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-20 · EIP-721 (NFT): https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-721
- BIP-39 (mnemonic): https://github.com/bitcoin/bips/blob/master/bip-0039.mediawiki · BIP-32: https://github.com/bitcoin/bips/blob/master/bip-0032.mediawiki
