## 🏛️ FOB — Exam Strategy

> [!warning] Highest weight: Unit 3 (30%)
> Master Solidity syntax, gas mechanism, and Remix deployment flow.

**"Write short notes" favorites:**
- CAP theorem
- UTXO vs Account model
- PoW vs PoS comparison
- 51% attack
- Oracle problem
- Re-entrancy vulnerability
- Self-Sovereign Identity (SSI)

**Practical viva tip:** Know the *why* behind each code line, not just the output.
