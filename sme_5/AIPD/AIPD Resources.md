# AIPD — Curated Resources

**AI Product Design (DI05016021)** · Curated links: official docs, free courses, tools, books, videos.

- Microsoft Guidelines for Human-AI Interaction: https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/guidelines-for-human-ai-interaction/
- OWASP LLM Top 10 (LLM security threats): https://owasp.org/www-project-top-10-for-large-language-model-applications/
- OWASP API Security Top 10: https://owasp.org/www-project-api-security/
- NIST AI Risk Management Framework: https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework
- OpenAI platform docs: https://platform.openai.com/docs · OpenAI pricing: https://openai.com/api/pricing/

2. *AI Engineering* — Chip Huyen (O'Reilly, 2025)
3. *Designing Data-Intensive Applications* — Martin Kleppmann (O'Reilly, 2017)

6. *Lean Product and Lean Analytics* — Ben Yoskovitz & Alistair Croll (O'Reilly, 2019) — maps to Unit 3
